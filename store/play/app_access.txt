No account or host is needed to review Muxa.

Muxa connects over SSH to the user's own Mac or Linux machine, where the muxa daemon runs the coding agents. To review it without one, the app has a built-in demo server with sample Tasks, Inbox items, Routines, documents and a conversation. It runs on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Open the app. The Hosts list is empty.
2. Tap "Try the Demo". Home opens with the Inbox.
3. Try:
   - Inbox: tap "Review fix-login", then Approve. The item leaves the list.
   - Capture: in the field above the Inbox, type "reply Sam about the launch #personal due today" and add it. It goes on top as a Reply.
   - Needs you: tap the waiting agent. Focus opens its terminal, which asks to allow edits to README.md.
   - In Focus, tap Chat: the agent's conversation and an approval card. Tap Allow. The terminal shows the answer.
   - In Focus, tap Reader: the Task's PLAN.md, and its Changes as diffs.
   - In Focus, tap Loop, then Start. Tap Loop again, then Stop.
   - On Home, tap Routines, pick a Routine, then tap Run Now.
   - With a hardware keyboard: Ctrl+Shift+H and Ctrl+Shift+F switch between Home and Focus; in Focus, Ctrl+Shift+R and Ctrl+Shift+C open the Reader and Chat.
4. To leave, tap "Demo" at the top of Home, then Disconnect. The empty Hosts list comes back.

A real connection needs a Mac or Linux machine with herdr-core and muxa-bridge (free, open source): https://github.com/xdgetech/muxa#setup
