No account or host is needed to review Muxa.

Muxa connects over SSH to the reviewer's own machine, where tmux runs. To review it without one, the app has a built-in demo with sample sessions. It runs on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Open the app. The host list is empty.
2. Tap "Try the Demo". It opens a sample agent session in Chat.
3. Try:
   - Chat: the agent waits on a question. Tap an answer card, or type in the composer.
   - The Agent and Shell switches above the chat show the terminal panes.
   - The sidebar lists the sessions by repo, with each agent's state. Tap another session.
   - Documents opens the Reader: the plan with a diagram, the changes as diffs, and images. It is the page button in the pane's bar, or an item in its "..." menu on a phone.
   - About 10 seconds in, a banner says that an agent in another session waits.
   - With a hardware keyboard: Cmd+K switches session, Cmd+J switches agent and terminal.
4. "Leave Demo" in the sidebar goes back to the empty host list.

A real connection needs a Mac or Linux machine with tmux and muxa-bridge (free, open source): https://github.com/xdgetech/muxa#setup
