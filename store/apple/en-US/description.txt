Muxa lets you run and follow your AI coding agents from your iPad or iPhone. The agents work on your own Mac or Linux machine. Muxa shows what needs you, each agent's terminal at its real size, and what the agents wrote.

ONE INBOX, IN YOUR ORDER
Home keeps what you have to do in one list: reviews, replies, decisions, failures and your own todos. Type a line such as "reply Anna about the launch #personal due fri", and Muxa reads the kind, the workspace and the date. New items go on top. Drag them into your order, and swipe Done or Later. A Later item comes back on top at the time you pick.

SEE WHO NEEDS YOU
Needs you lists the agents that wait on you right now, by the state each agent reports itself. One tap opens that agent's terminal.

THE TERMINAL, AT ITS REAL SIZE
Focus shows one Task's terminal at the size of the screen you look at it on. Beside it, one panel at a time: the Inbox, the Reader or Chat.

CHAT WITH AN AGENT
Chat shows a Claude Code agent's conversation from its own records, with a line to send it a message. When the agent asks for approval, a card shows the answers its menu offers. You pick one. Muxa never answers for you.

READ WHAT YOUR AGENTS WRITE
The Reader shows a Task folder's Markdown and HTML documents, its changed files and the branch's commits as diffs, and its images. It stays current while it is open.

TASK LOOPS
Turn on the Loop, and a check such as "make check" runs after each finished turn of the agent. A failed check asks the agent to fix the cause. A pass puts a Review item in your Inbox.

ROUTINES
Routines run an agent on your machine on a schedule, for example at 8:00 on weekdays. Their briefings reach your Inbox. See each Routine's next run and last result, and run one now.

BUILT FOR THE KEYBOARD, AND FOR TOUCH
With a hardware keyboard, Cmd+Shift+H opens Home, Cmd+Shift+F opens Focus, Cmd+P goes to a Task, and Cmd+N adds to the Inbox. Without one, a bar adds Esc, Tab, Ctrl and the arrows to the terminal. Japanese input works in the terminal.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Muxa connects over SSH directly to your machine. There is no Muxa server and no account, and Muxa collects no data. Its key is made on the device and is restricted on the host to muxa-bridge.

LOOK AROUND FIRST
A built-in sample workspace, with Tasks, Inbox items and Routines, works without a host. Tap it on the first screen.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A Mac or Linux machine with herdr-core, git and an SSH server.
- muxa-bridge and its daemon, free and open source, installed on that machine: https://github.com/xdgetech/muxa#setup
- A way to reach it, for example over your local network or a VPN such as Tailscale.

Muxa works with agents such as Claude Code and Codex by running their official command-line tools on your own machine. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Anthropic or OpenAI, and it never handles their sign-in.
